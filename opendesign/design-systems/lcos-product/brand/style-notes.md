# Visual foundations

## Material

The Canvas is a quiet cool-neutral field. App chrome uses porcelain-white translucent surfaces, a fine hairline and diffuse shadow. Blur belongs to local overlays, not the whole screen.

## Object identity

Content remains the strongest signal: image, document page, conversation spine, run machine or decision/checkpoint. Category color only supports the shape through a fine edge, glyph, compact tag or ambient light.

## Spacing and targets

Use the 4/8/12/16/24 scale. Desktop icon controls are normally 32px or larger; primary compact controls target 36px. Keep 6–8px between adjacent actions.

## States

- Hover: subtle cool-neutral fill and a small elevation increase.
- Pressed: 0.97–0.985 scale, no delayed response.
- Selected: purple → blue → cyan thin rim; no full-card color fill.
- Running: at most one or two restrained moving edges.
- Waiting / review / failed: static semantic indicator plus readable text in the relevant rail.

## Motion

Use 140–220ms with restrained ease-out. Spatial navigation may use a soft spring. Disable nonessential motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
