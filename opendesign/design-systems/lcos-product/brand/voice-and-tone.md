# Voice and tone

- Use concise, concrete Chinese by default; preserve familiar file and creative-work terms.
- Tell users what is happening and what they can do next. Do not expose internal architecture as onboarding copy.
- Prefer `待确认`, `执行中`, `需要输入`, `已保存`, `来源已变化` over raw enum strings.
- Use sentence case. Avoid decorative punctuation, emoji and hype.
- Buttons begin with an action when space allows: `导入`, `定位内容`, `重试`, `接受为当前版本`.
- Destructive actions name the object and consequence before confirmation.
